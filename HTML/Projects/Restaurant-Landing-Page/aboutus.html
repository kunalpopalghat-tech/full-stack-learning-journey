About Sunny's World

Sunny's World is designed as an expansive nature-inspired retreat for tourists, families,
and corporate groups. Located just 15 to 20 minutes from Pune city, the property spans
100 acres of lush greenery.

Key Highlights & Facilities:

  • The Tiger Eye Adventure Resort: Known as one of the largest adventure parks in
    India, it features over 100 extreme and family-friendly activities like zip-lining, ATV
    rides, paintball, and giant swings.
  • Luxury Stays: The resort offers uniquely styled, premium accommodations named
    after precious stones, including Moonstone Swiss Chalets, Topaz Luxurious Glass
    Tents, and Garnet Spa Villas.
  • Event Spaces: With 11 elegant banquet halls and 7 sprawling hilltop lawns, it is a
    premium destination for destination weddings and large-scale corporate
    events.
  • Dining Options: Guests can enjoy gourmet multi-cuisine meals across the resort's
    restaurants, including The Ruby Hilltop Restaurant and The Shiv-Lingam Pure Veg
    Restaurant.
  • Sports Kingdom: A cutting-edge sports complex featuring courts for badminton,
    tennis, squash, and a swimming pool.
